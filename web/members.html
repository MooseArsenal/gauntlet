SCIM provisioning — let the identity provider create and deactivate members

Okta, Entra ID, OneLogin and JumpCloud provision users over SCIM 2.0. Mint a token, paste it and the base URL into the provider's SCIM connector. The token is shown once; only its hash is kept. Minting again rotates it.

Copy both now. Deprovisioning through the provider deactivates the member and ends their sessions; the record keeps its author.
